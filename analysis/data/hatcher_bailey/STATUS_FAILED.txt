Why the cascade ended without a CSV
------------------------------------
* Hatcher 1986 covers Christ Church monks 1395-1505 only — single
  site, no 1348 anchor possible, annual CDR locked in Figure 1.
* Campbell 2016 cites cropyields.ac.uk for crop yields (not
  mortality); supplementary mortality file does not exist publicly.
* Kelly-Ó Gráda 2014 uses Page (2003) micro-data archived at the
  UK Data Service (Study 4086) under registration-gated terms —
  not openly downloadable.
* Hatcher-Bailey 2001 is a book; no online appendix or
  machine-readable mortality table accompanies it.

Reproducibility note
--------------------
A future researcher with institutional UKDS credentials could
re-attempt Tier B by registering for Study 4086, downloading
the 14 tab-delimited Winchester pipe-roll files, aggregating
the entry-fines into annual death-proxies by manor (per Kelly
& Ó Gráda 2014 methodology), and reconstructing a Winchester
manor-year inheritance panel for 1263-1349. That panel would
permit the 1348 anchor test but would still cover only the
Winchester bishopric (60 manors, one estate, pre-1350).
