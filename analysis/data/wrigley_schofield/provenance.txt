=== Cascade tier C (Schofield 2022 PRA-2 OCR): FAILED (2026-05-15) ===
---------------------------------------------------------------------

PDF download: SUCCESS.
  URL: http://www.localpopulationstudies.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/
       Parish-register-aggregate-analyses-SECOND-EDITION-PUBLISHED.pdf
  HTTP 200, 1,012,617 bytes, 45 pages, PDF 1.7, downloaded cleanly.
  Saved as schofield_2022_pra2.pdf.

Content inspection (pdfplumber, all 45 pages):
  Pages 1-3: front matter, title, copyright.
  Pages 4-5: contents and list of tables/figures.
  Pages 6-23: narrative on the database, how it was assembled, what
              the parish-level files contain (bap_10, bur_10, mar_10,
              bap_50, bur_50, mar_50, etc.), quality of registration,
              use cases (seasonality, fluctuations, crisis mortality,
              long-run trends).
  Page 24:   Figure 1 - map of the 404 parishes.
  Pages 25-34: Table 2 - alphabetic list of 404 parishes with their
              1811 populations.
  Pages 35-45: Table 3 - same 404 parishes ordered by county.

Critical finding: this PDF is the *introductory guide* to the PRA-2
parish-level CD-ROM database.  It does NOT contain any national annual
back-projected CBR, CDR, or population time-series tables, and there is
no Appendix A3.

Page 18 of the PDF says explicitly: "annual national totals of baptisms,
burials and marriages estimated from the aggregate of the 404 parishes
can be found in the population history of england" - i.e. these series
live in the Wrigley-Schofield 1981 hardcopy book (Appendix A3), not in
this 2022 guide PDF.

Additional source survey (web searches and webfetches):
  - https://archive.org/details/populationhistor0000wrig_w4s3
      The 1981 W-S book on Internet Archive is "access-restricted-item"
      with "cut text on some pages".  Borrow-only, no direct PDF
      download, OCR not externally available.
  - https://www.campop.geog.cam.ac.uk/datasets/
      Lists only 404pars.csv (parish metadata, not annual vital rates).
      Annual CBR/CDR series are reachable only via the UK Data Archive
      (UKDS) doi:10.5255/UKDA-SN-853082, which requires academic
      registration (cascade tier B - out of scope without credentials).
  - GitHub gist / public CSV search: no public reproduction of the W-S
      Appendix A3 annual series found.

OCR was therefore not attempted on the Schofield 2022 PDF, because the
target tables are not in that PDF.  Running pdfplumber.extract_tables()
on pages 25-45 would only retrieve the 1811 parish population list
(Tables 2 and 3), which does not provide annual national CBR/CDR.
