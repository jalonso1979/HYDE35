# Critical Review — *Good genes, good weather and a prosperous land: A re-examination of the deep root factors of economic development* (Alonso Ortiz & Da-Rocha)

## Verdict

The paper's **core machinery is sound and the central empirical engine is publishable in principle.** Across six independent lenses the grouped Shapley-Owen decomposition was verified as correctly implemented: the efficiency identity (∑ φ_s = R²_full − R²_baseline) holds to machine precision, coalition players (climate as 4 columns, the functional-allele bundle as 8) are added and removed jointly with no double-counting, the 5!=120-ordering weighting is the standard closed form, the panel join produces 196 unique ISO3 keys with no silent NA coercion, and the unit-test suite (72 tests) carries real numeric asserts. The DARC graded-partialling numbers and the FADS1/2-sharpening result reproduce to six decimals. This is careful work.

**The single biggest threat to the paper is that the manuscript's central inferential section (§4.4 / §5.4, the Westfall-Young FWER paragraph at horserace.tex:298) describes a *superseded version of the analysis that no longer exists in the code*.** It reports five FWER survivors and attributes two of them to "predicted heterozygosity" with F-statistics (11.33, 10.82) that appear nowhere in any committed output — a substrate the paper itself demoted out of the principal matrix at line 120. The actual code produces a different survivor set (the two extra survivors are the Neolithic-ancestry fraction), and crucially **no per-capita-income cell survives FWER at all** (the headlined functional-allele/GDPpc cell is F=8.46, p_adj_wy=0.066). This is not a typo: it is a verbatim carryover from the prior "horserace v2" panel, and it propagates into the abstract, intro, and discussion as the inferential backbone of the "temporal partition." A referee re-running `robustness_battery.py` gets a different result than the paper claims. Every one of the seven review lenses independently flagged this.

The second structural concern is **a metric-switching artifact in the headline narrative**: the clean "each chapter has its protagonist" partition holds under *no single criterion*. Climate (density-1500) and the functional bundle (GDPpc) are assigned dominance on Shapley R² — where they win but fail FWER — while ancestral yield and the Neolithic fraction are assigned dominance on the WY F-test — where they survive but lose on Shapley (the functional bundle is the Shapley argmax on 4 of 6 outcomes, and ancestral yield/Neolithic each win *zero* Shapley cells). The body partially signposts this (lines 49, 299, 552), so it is a major framing/disclosure problem rather than a blocker, but it directly contradicts the prior internal note that "ancestral crop yield wins 3 of 4 outcomes" on Shapley. Finally, the DARC "65% attenuation by state history" — one of three named contributions — is a sample-composition artifact (an 18-country drop, not channel displacement) and must be re-run on a fixed sample. None of these defects touches the underlying estimator; all are fixable by rewriting prose to match the shipped code and re-running two robustness ladders on consistent samples. The paper is salvageable, but in its current state the inferential section is non-reproducible and two of its three named contributions are over-claimed.

## Blockers

**1. The Westfall-Young FWER section reports survivors from a deleted substrate; no income cell actually survives.** (horserace.tex:298, §4.4/§5.4; verified against `analysis/data/deep_determinants/robustness_battery.parquet`, `robustness_battery.py:41-62,204-261`)

This was raised independently by all seven lenses (ids: `section54-stale-v2-fwer-survivors`, `wy-stale-predicted-het-survivors`, `hr-wy-stale-hpred-survivors` ×2, `hr-wy-survivors-stale-narrative`, `hr-wy-h-pred-stale`) and confirmed at blocker severity each time.

- **What:** The paragraph states "Predicted heterozygosity carries the remaining two [survivors]: on log GDP per capita 2015 (F=11.33, p_WY=0.015), reproducing the Ashraf-Galor (2013) principal finding... and on log population growth (F=10.82, p_WY=0.019)." Predicted heterozygosity is not a substrate in the v3 WY matrix (`SCALAR_SUBSTRATES = [neolithic_frac, ancestral_yield_log, pandemic_intensity_norm]` plus the `climate_bundle` and `functional_alleles` coalitions). The F-values 11.33 and 10.82 exist nowhere in the output (searched the [10.5, 12.0] band: empty).
- **Why it matters:** This is the section that declares which cells are statistically defensible after multiplicity correction — the paper's entire inferential claim. Two of five named survivors are fictitious, two genuine survivors (Neolithic fraction on density-2025 and dt-timing) are omitted, the "reproduces Ashraf-Galor on GDPpc" claim is false, and the family-size count is given as 24 (it is 30).
- **Evidence:** The actual five survivors (p_adj_wy<0.05) are: `ancestral_yield_log`→`log_pop_growth` (F=26.97, p=0.000), `ancestral_yield_log`→`log_popd_1500` (F=15.55, p=0.002), `ancestral_yield_log`→`log_popd_2025` (F=10.40, p=0.030), `neolithic_frac`→`log_popd_2025` (F=17.86, p=0.000), `neolithic_frac`→`dt_timing_year` (F=13.21, p=0.005). `functional_alleles`→`log_gdppc_2015` is F=8.46, p=0.066 — does **not** survive.
- **Fix:** Regenerate the entire paragraph from `robustness_battery.parquet`. Report the five real survivors (3 ancestral-yield density/growth cells + 2 Neolithic-fraction cells), state explicitly that the functional-bundle/GDPpc cell is F=8.46, p_WY=0.066 and does not survive, delete every "predicted heterozygosity" and "reproducing Ashraf-Galor" reference, and reconcile the family size to 30. Then propagate the corrected survivor set through lines 122, 167, 338, 508, 512, 527, 536, 541, 550.

**2. The DARC "65% attenuation by state history" is a sample-composition artifact, not channel displacement.** (horserace.tex:29, 53, 414, 471-503 / Table 12; `exercise_colonial_partial_extended.py:85-118`)

(id: `darc-statehist-sample-artifact`, confirmed blocker)

- **What:** The paper claims the Bockstette-Putterman state-history index "absorbs essentially all of DARC's negative loading — a 65 percent attenuation" (−2.65 → +0.94) and reinterprets DARC as "an imperfect proxy for low pre-1500 state capacity." But adding `state_hist` silently drops 18 countries (N=154 → N=136) via `dropna` over the spec's own regressors. **On the fixed 136-country sample, DARC is already +0.62 (p=0.667, wrong sign) *before* `state_hist` enters.** Of the total +3.58 swing, 91% (+3.27) is the sample drop and only 9% (+0.32) is the actual conditioning.
- **Why it matters:** This is one of three named contributions (abstract, intro, discussion). "Attenuation toward zero" is not even coherent when the base coefficient on the relevant sample is already +0.62 and far from significant. `state_hist` is itself insignificant (β=−0.49, p=0.21) and corr(DARC, state_hist)=−0.49 — the signature of collinearity-driven instability. The stored parquet records N=136 for the +state_hist rows while Table 12's footnote (line 496) asserts "N=154 in all rows" — a direct factual misstatement.
- **Fix:** Re-run the entire graded-partialling ladder on a single fixed sample (the 136 countries with non-missing `state_hist`) so every column has identical N. If DARC is already insignificant/positive on that sample before any partialling, drop the channel-displacement claim and report that DARC's negative GDPpc loading is carried by the African + Gulf countries lacking state-history coverage. Correct the "N=154 in all rows" footnote. Also note the raw-territory `state_hist` vs ancestry-weighted DARC measurement mismatch.

**3. Two headline dominance claims contradict the paper's own Shapley table.** (horserace.tex:29, 49, 218, 220, 548; `tab04_shapley_table.tex`, `exercise1_shapley_results.parquet`)

This was raised by five lenses (ids: `shapley-prose-contradicts-own-table`, `hr-dominance-neolithic-vs-functional`, `hr-neolithic-not-dominant-d2025`, `hr-shapley-neolithic-density2025-false`, `hr-abstract-popd2025-dominance`). The skeptics split on whether to call it blocker or major — I treat it as **blocker** because the false parenthetical is reader-facing and one part is contradicted within the same section.

- **What:** Line 220 states Neolithic's "Shapley R² is 0.039 on log population density 2025 (the largest substrate contribution on this outcome)." It is not: the table printed on the same page shows `functional_alleles` = 0.064 > `neolithic_frac` = 0.039. On dt-timing, `functional_alleles` = 0.122 ≫ `neolithic_frac` = 0.032 (~4×). The abstract (line 29), intro (line 49), and discussion (line 548) all assign these two margins to the Neolithic fraction by "dominates"/"largest"/"strongest individual contributor." Worse, line 218 *correctly* states the functional bundle "dominates... demographic-transition timing... 0.122, also the largest" — directly contradicting line 220 and the abstract within the same paper.
- **Why it matters:** The "temporal partition" is the paper's central organizing claim. For two of six outcomes the substrate assignment is false against the very Shapley statistic the text cites, and the functional bundle is the Shapley argmax on 4 of 6 outcomes (collapsing the "one substrate per moment" story under that metric).
- **Fix:** Replace line 220's parenthetical with the correct basis: Neolithic is the **only WY-surviving substrate** on these two cells (p_adj_wy = 0.000 and 0.005) while the functional bundle fails (p = 0.692, 0.143); it is *not* the largest Shapley contribution. State the dominance criterion (WY-survival vs Shapley R²) explicitly for each headline cell. Reconcile line 218 with the abstract.

## Major issues

**1. The "temporal partition" is built by switching the dominance criterion cell-by-cell.** (horserace.tex:29, 49, 546-565; `exercise1_shapley_results.parquet`, `robustness_battery.parquet`) (id: `hr-partition-metric-switch`, downgraded blocker→major because the body partially discloses it.)

No single metric yields the four-pillar partition. On Shapley R², the per-outcome winners are climate / functional / functional / climate / functional / functional — ancestral yield and Neolithic win **zero** outcomes. On the WY F-test, only ancestral yield and Neolithic survive — climate (max F=2.14) and the functional bundle (GDPpc F=8.46, p=0.066) survive nothing. So climate→density-1500 and functional→GDPpc rest on Shapley (and fail WY), while ancestral→growth/density and Neolithic→density-2025/dt rest on WY (and lose on Shapley). The body does signpost this (line 299: climate cells "do not survive FWER... largest F at 2.14"; line 49 distinguishes "largest joint statistic" from "largest variance contribution"), so it is not undisclosed fabrication — but the abstract and conclusion flatten both metrics into a uniform "dominates," obscuring which pillar rests on which criterion. **Fix:** report both metrics for every headline cell; reframe to "climate and the functional bundle carry the largest Shapley shares on their cells but are not FWER-robust; ancestral yield and Neolithic are FWER-robust but never the largest Shapley share."

**2. The descriptives caption claims a 185-country analysis sample; the principal sample is 162.** (horserace.tex:178, 536; `tab01_descriptives.tex`) (ids: `hr-sample-n-162-vs-185`, `hr-185-vs-162-sample`, confirmed major)

The Table 1 caption asserts "The 185-country sub-panel... is the analysis sample for the principal exercises," and §6 (line 536) refers to "the full 185-country panel," but lines 81 and 560 correctly say 162. The functional-allele bundle binds the principal sample at exactly 162 (verified: all-five-substrate intersection = 162; the table body itself shows N=162 for every functional-allele row). 185 is exactly `H_pred_pwadj.notna()` — a stale count from the pre-functional-bundle v2 build. The caption is internally contradicted by its own table. **Fix:** replace 185 with 162 at lines 178 and 536; note per-outcome listwise N varies (density 194, GDPpc 161, DT-timing 144); fix the `exercise_genetic_controls.py` "~185 countries" comment.

**3. The mediation suppressor structure is misattributed to a nonexistent row and the "well-behaved climate bundle" framing is contradicted by its own table.** (horserace.tex:262, 267, 279, 284, 293; `tab05_mediation_table.tex`, `fig04_mediation.py:5-10`, `exercise2_mediation_results.parquet`) (ids: `hr-mediation-suppressor-reframe`, `hr-mediation-suppressor-misattribution`, `hr-mediation-failed-spin`, confirmed major)

The prose says the suppressor / out-of-[0,1] structure is "concentrated in the heterozygosity and pandemic rows," but heterozygosity is not a row in the current 5×6 matrix. The actual 11/30 out-of-[0,1] cells span pandemic (5), Neolithic (3), functional (2), and climate (1) — including the climate-bundle/GDPpc cell (−0.92), which directly undercuts the repeated "climate bundle is well-mediated" framing. The figure-code header (`fig04_mediation.py:5-10`) preserves the original honest assessment ("suppressor effects in MOST cells... the one clean, interpretable mediation is pandemic→GDPpc"). The fig caption (line 267) even lists the density-2025 climate cell (share 0.17, CI [−1.60, 0.74] crossing zero, unbolded in Table 5) among "well-mediated" cells. This is the prior internal "failed mediation / 14-16 suppressor" finding reframed; the table footnote discloses it numerically, but the prose launders it onto a removed substrate. (Note: the *sharpest* version of the spin claim — "only ~2-3 cells bounded away from zero" — is itself slightly off; 6 cells have CIs strictly excluding zero. Use that corrected count.) **Fix:** rewrite the mediation prose to describe the actual current rows; state the 11/30 out-of-[0,1] / 6-of-30 CI-excludes-zero counts up front; correct the line-267 caption; drop all "heterozygosity row" attributions and the nonexistent "share 3.30" cell.

**4. "Functional-allele bundle dominates per-capita income" is presented as part of an FWER-defensible partition, but that cell fails FWER.** (horserace.tex:29, 49, 218, 554; `robustness_battery.parquet`) (id: `functional-bundle-gdppc-not-fwer`, confirmed major)

The income leg is the cell that fails the gate (F=8.46, p_adj_wy=0.066) while line 51 frames WY as "the joint testing scale at which the temporal-moment pattern emerges as a statistically defensible structure." The bundle has the largest Shapley R² on GDPpc (0.175), which is a true descriptive statement, but the income margin is also the entry point for the entire DARC/state-history partialling section. **Fix:** state explicitly that the GDPpc cell carries the largest Shapley share but is borderline under FWER (p=0.066, does not survive); soften "dominates per-capita income" to a variance-share statement. (Largely subsumed by Blocker 1's rewrite.)

**5. The Westfall-Young procedure is single-step maxT (not the "step-down" claimed) over raw F-statistics with heterogeneous numerator df.** (horserace.tex:298; `robustness_battery.py:90-101, 230-248`) (id: `wy-maxt-not-stepdown-rawF`, confirmed major)

Two defects: (a) the code implements single-step maxT (one fixed null max-distribution for every hypothesis), not the step-down algorithm named in the text — a mislabel; single-step is uniformly more conservative. (b) More seriously, the per-permutation maximum is taken over **raw F-statistics with incomparable null distributions** (numerator df = 1 for scalars, 4 for climate, 8 for the functional bundle). An 8-df F at per-cell p=0.001 is only F≈3.5 while a 1-df F at the same p is ≈11.3, so the family max is dominated by the fat-tailed low-df scalar cells, systematically under-testing the high-df bundle. Every survivor is a scalar substrate; the functional bundle never survives (best p=0.066). This plausibly explains the GDPpc non-survival mechanically rather than substantively. The F-test also uses classical OLS SEs (line 92) while the headline coefficient table uses HC3 (`exercise1_shapley.py:89`). **Fix:** use minP (convert each F to a p-value via its own F(df_num, df_den) reference, take min p across cells per permutation) or studentize; relabel as single-step maxT or implement genuine step-down; use HC3 to match the coefficient table.

**6. Non-deterministic bootstrap seeds.** (`exercise2_mediation.py:49`; `robustness_battery.py:123,359`; `exercise3_climate_clusters.py:57`; `exercise2_subsample_stability.py:63`) (ids: `hr-hash-seed-nonrepro` ×2 — one skeptic downgraded to minor, one held at major; I keep **major** because it affects every published CI and the cell-bolding rule.)

Bootstrap seeds are `abs(hash((outcome, substrate_key))) % 2**31`; Python salts tuple-of-string hashing via `PYTHONHASHSEED`, which is unset anywhere in the repo. Verified: consecutive interpreters return different seeds, and re-running the climate/GDPpc cell under fresh seeds yields CIs differing from the committed parquet ([−4.31, 0.73] vs [−4.60, 0.76] etc.). Point estimates are deterministic; CIs and `n_boot_valid` counts are not. The WY permutation (rng=46) and LOO (seed=0) are correctly fixed. **Fix:** replace `hash()` with a stable hash (`hashlib.sha256` or `zlib.crc32` of the key) or an explicit seed dict, export `PYTHONHASHSEED`, and regenerate the affected parquets/figures/tables.

**7. Pervasive cell-count drift (24 vs 30) in prose and docstrings, including the FWER family size.** (horserace.tex:262, 289, 298, 527 vs 51, 55, 308, 322; `robustness_battery.py:5-9,205`; `exercise1_shapley.py:8`; `exercise2_mediation.py:5`; `figA_robustness_battery.py:5`) (ids: `hr-cell-count-24-vs-30`, `hr-stale-cell-counts`, `hr-cell-count-24-vs-30-drift`, `hr-cell-count-inconsistency` — skeptics split minor/major; I keep **major** because line 298 states a *wrong FWER denominator*, 24, which is load-bearing for the correction.)

The matrix is unambiguously 5 substrates × 6 outcomes = 30 cells (every parquet has 30 rows; the WY max is taken over all 30). The text and docstrings oscillate between 30 and stale 24/16/4×4/4×6 framings, several still listing `H_pred_pwadj` as a substrate. The WY computation itself is correct over 30; only the stated denominator is wrong. **Fix:** global replace to 5-substrate / 30-cell; fix the `robustness_battery.py:5-9` and `figA_robustness_battery.py:5` docstrings (which still name H_pred); update `exercise1_shapley.py:8` and `exercise2_mediation.py:5`.

## Minor issues & robustness gaps

- **Shapley shares carry no confidence intervals** (`shapley.py:45-46` returns raw `rsquared`; `exercise1_shapley.py` emits point estimates only). A country bootstrap shows the density-2025 (functional vs Neolithic), growth (functional vs ancestral, a near-dead-heat 0.0962 vs 0.0957), and density-1500 (climate vs functional) margins all straddle zero — those orderings are within sampling noise. *However*, the two marquee cells (functional→GDPpc, difference-CI [+0.09, +0.26]; functional→dt-timing [+0.04, +0.16]) bootstrap cleanly, so the partition is not uniformly coin-flip. Add difference-CIs to the Shapley table and flag the within-noise cells. (id: `shapley-no-ci-rankings-within-noise`, partially confirmed → minor)
- **Shapley uses raw in-sample R² with 24 regressors on n=118-162; no adjusted-R² or out-of-sample check.** A placebo shows pure-noise regressors inflate R² ~8× more for the 8-column functional bundle than for a scalar — biasing the comparison toward the bundle players the paper crowns. Repeat with adjusted/cross-validated R² and report whether the pattern survives, or at minimum note the bias and column counts in the caption. (id: `no-adj-r2-no-oos-overfitting`, confirmed minor)
- **Ancestral-yield/climate residualisation under-corrects.** `A` is residualised only on (T̄, P̄), but the climate bundle is four elements (incl. σ_v^T, σ_v^P — the latter being the *dominant* primitive for density-1500). The incremental linear R² from the volatility components is 0.073 vs 0.069 for the two means — i.e. the residualisation leaves in about half the linear overlap, before the nonlinear GAEZ overlap. Residualise on all four bundle members and re-report. (id: `ancestral-yield-climate-endogeneity-undertreated`, confirmed minor)
- **Pathway-mediation lacks stated identification assumptions.** The mediator (pathway) is a k-means cluster of the same climate primitives that constitute the climate-bundle substrate, so the climate "mediation share" is partly a mechanical decomposition of one climate object into a clustered version of itself; sequential-ignorability and temporal ordering are neither stated nor tested, and continent-FE validity is asserted ("the principal mediation result for the suppressor cells") rather than demonstrated. State the assumptions; justify continent FE as sensitivity. (id: `mediation-assumptions-and-suppressor-framing`, confirmed)
- **"DARC attenuated to insignificance" over-claim** (lines 53, 108, 554): the baseline DARC coefficient is already insignificant (−2.65, p=0.113), so it cannot be "rendered" insignificant. Reword to "flips the sign of an already-insignificant baseline coefficient." (Lines 29 and 565 are fine.) (id: `hr-darc-attenuated-to-insignificance`, confirmed minor)
- **One genuinely over-reaching causal clause** at line 55: "recovers the major existing causal-identification results of the literature as the cells in which the channel they identify is the locally-dominant substrate." Reword to "localises each literature's preferred channel as the locally-dominant variance contributor." The broader "pervasive causal language" charge was *refuted* — the paper's prevailing register ("dominates," "is the dominant predictor," "contributes unique-variance weight") is correctly variance-share language, the DARC material is explicitly anti-causal, and the line-559/560 caveat is prominent. This is a one-clause fix, not a paper-wide problem. (id: `causal-verbs-over-variance-decomposition`, partially confirmed → minor)
- **Functional-allele coverage silently drops 34 countries** (incl. Taiwan, Iceland), traceable to the Putterman-Weil destination-row gap. Document the dropped set in Appendix A. Note: the reviewer's framing that this "drives the cut to 162" is overstated — the no-fa five-substrate sample is already 174, and Taiwan is also missing H_pred, so the fa bundle uniquely removes only 12 countries. (id: `hr-taiwan-iceland-fa-missing`, partially confirmed → minor)
- **WY F-test uses homoskedastic SEs** while the OLS table uses HC3; unused dead `hypotheses` string at `robustness_battery.py:94`. Low impact (the permutation is internally consistent). Use HC3 consistently or add a one-line note; delete the dead string. (id: `hr-wy-fstat-nonrobust-se`, partially confirmed → minor)
- **Shapley and WY run on slightly different samples** (joint-listwise n=162 vs per-cell n=175-194). This is normal, disclosed listwise behavior, not a defect — but a one-sentence flag that dominance and significance calls use slightly different country sets would help. (id: `shapley-wy-different-samples`, partially confirmed → minor/transparency)

## Code & reproducibility

**What works:** The estimator core is genuinely solid. `shapley.py` correctly averages marginal R² over orderings with proper Owen weights, handles coalition players, and enforces a single common listwise-deleted sample per decomposition (lines 78-79). `mediation.py` implements both β-attenuation and partial-R² shares with a sensible degenerate-denominator guard. The master join (`build_horserace_panel.py`) is clean: 196 unique ISO3 keys, no duplicates, sensible per-substrate coverage. The 72-test suite passes in ~4s and the estimator unit tests carry real numeric asserts (Shapley efficiency to 1e-6, coalition identity, mediation pure/no-mediation cases). The WY permutation itself (max over 30 cells, fixed rng=46) is correctly applied — the *computation* is right even where the *reported numbers* are stale.

**What's broken / stale:** (1) The shipped output parquets and the paper's §4.4 prose describe different analyses — the prose is v2 (H_pred-based, 24-cell), the code is v3 (functional+Neolithic, 30-cell). A replicator running `robustness_battery.py` gets a *different FWER survivor set* than the paper claims (Blocker 1). (2) `state_hist` rows in the colonial-partial output run on N=136 while the table footnote says N=154 (Blocker 2). (3) Multiple code docstrings still list `H_pred_pwadj` as a principal substrate (`robustness_battery.py:5-9`, `figA_robustness_battery.py:5`). (4) Bootstrap CIs are non-reproducible run-to-run (Major 6).

**What a replicator would hit:** No reproduction entry point exists for paper5 — no Makefile, `run_all`, or `reproduce_horserace.sh` (the companion long_shadow project ships one). `build_horserace_panel.py` consumes 8 upstream `build_*.py` parquets and the exercise modules import constants from `exercise1_shapley`, so the DAG must be reverse-engineered. Combined with the unsalted seeds, even correct ordering won't reproduce the committed CIs. Tests smoke-test output shape (`len(df)==30`, CI ordering) but never assert the WY survivor set, any paper number, or that H_pred is absent from outputs — which is precisely why the §4.4 regression went undetected. (ids: `hr-no-repro-entrypoint`, `hr-tests-dont-pin-survivors`, confirmed minor)
